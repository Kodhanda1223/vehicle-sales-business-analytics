# Vehicle Sales Business Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

Vehicle Sales Business Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing used vehicle sales, pricing patterns, customer demand, and regional performance.

The project uses Python, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw vehicle sales data into meaningful business insights and interactive dashboards.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall vehicle sales performance
- Identify top-performing vehicle brands and models
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Understand the relationship between mileage and selling price
- Compare market reference price with actual selling price
- Analyze vehicle condition and pricing
- Identify sales trends over time
- Identify potential pricing opportunities
- Provide actionable business recommendations
