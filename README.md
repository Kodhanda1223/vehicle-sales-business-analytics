# Vehicle Sales Business Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

Vehicle Sales Business Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing vehicle sales, pricing, mileage, market value, brands, and regional sales performance.

The project demonstrates how raw vehicle sales data can be transformed into meaningful business insights using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall vehicle sales performance
- Identify top-performing vehicle brands
- Compare selling price with market value
- Analyze sales performance by state
- Understand the relationship between mileage and selling price
- Analyze vehicle sales across different price ranges
- Create an interactive business dashboard
